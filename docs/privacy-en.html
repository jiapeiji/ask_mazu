Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What We Collect

  • Your name and city — used only to address you in results, stored locally, never uploaded.
  • Your throw records (question, result, sign) — stored on your device only, never uploaded.
  • Crash logs and basic usage statistics — collected anonymously to improve the app experience.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No real name, ID number, phone number, email, contacts, or location history.
  • No device identifiers such as IMEI, IDFA, or MAC address.
  • We never sell or share your personal data with any third party.

Third-Party SDKs

This app does not integrate advertising, analytics, or social sharing SDKs. With V1's App Store In-App Purchase integration, only Apple knows about your subscription purchase; the app itself does not access your Apple ID.

Your Rights

  • All your data lives on this device. You can wipe it at any time via Settings → Reset All Data.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.
  • For questions, contact us through the Feedback link in Settings.

Children

This app is suitable for all ages. Minors should have a guardian review this policy before use.
